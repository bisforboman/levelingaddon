Flight points and flight times (taxi-nodes.json, taxi-paths.json) are converted from Data/Taxi.lua of
https://github.com/cjber/shortest-path-forever at commit bc9eaa96154c319a3ab7ef30433e73b2c6b86839,
which is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. The flight masters and their map positions are QuestieDB's (GPL-3.0),
linked to the flight points with the zone rectangles in Data/Zones.lua of the same commit.
The header of Data/Taxi.lua follows as it stands there.

Generated by tools/gen_transit.py — do not edit.
Source: TaxiNodes/TaxiPath/TaxiPathNode; InFlight classic directed durations (seconds).
DB2: https://wago.tools/db2 (CSV build 1.60.1.70938).
DB: https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/tree/22b51464f1625f6ef6275771de1f5466c6f5d19e
InFlight: https://github.com/LudiusMaximus/InFlight/tree/2efdd7ba6d0e094edf84a04eeb40c038f004ea52

Speed: cmangos/mangos-classic src/game/MotionGenerators/PathMovementGenerator.cpp, TAXI_FLIGHT_SPEED.
