The card now carries only what cannot be drawn: the name of the thing to find, and a count. Step kind is an icon. The boxes tick themselves when the game says a step is done. Skipping is a separate, quiet control: three candidate designs are switchable below.

The whole vocabulary

Eight marks. The first two are the ones the game already puts over quest givers' heads, so there is nothing to learn. This legend is for this page; it is not shown in the game.

Where the words went

  • Accept, Turn in, Kill, Collect became the icon in front of the name.
  • Done is automatic: the count fills, the step ticks and fades. The player never marks anything done.
  • Skip is one cross at the end of the row under the pointer. A skipped step stays in the list and can be brought back later.
  • Quest name and place moved to the game's ordinary tooltip when the pointer rests on a row.
  • Distance is on the arrow only.
  • Settings is in the addon list by the minimap.
  • First login, hidden, dungeon messages are a line in chat, not on the card.

Planning ahead

At rest the card shows three steps: the current one and the next two. For a longer view there are two places to look, and neither adds anything to the screen while you play.

  • Timeline on the card. Clicking the card unrolls it into the next eight or so steps along a line, grouped under the place they happen in. Try the "Plan ahead" moment above.
  • Route on the world map. When you open the map, the next few stops are numbered and joined by a dashed line, so you can see the trip, not just the list.

Because the addon computes the route as you go, steps further out are a forecast: they can change if you level up, pick up another quest, or skip something. Later stops are drawn hollow for that reason.

A list you can scroll back through

The card is one continuous list of steps. Finished steps and skipped steps stay in it, in order, just above the current one. The card shows the current step and what follows; the mouse wheel scrolls back through what came before. A skipped step can be brought back at any time, not only in the first few seconds.

Cards are at true size. Progress is automatic (count, then a tick). The only things you can click are the cross on a pending step and the restore arrow on a skipped one.

How a skipped step differs from a finished one

Each card shows the same stretch of history: two finished steps, one skipped, one more finished, then the current step.

  • 1 · Crossed out. Keeps its own icon, name struck through. Reads as "not doing this" at a glance.
  • 2 · Dash. A dash where finished steps have a tick. Quietest; the difference is one small mark.
  • 3 · Skip mark. A double chevron where finished steps have a tick. Says "jumped over" rather than "cancelled".

Trade-offs to know about

  • Icons alone do not say which quest a turn-in is for. The tooltip covers it; players with two quests at one NPC may want the quest name back.
  • In a dungeon the card shrinks to a pause mark. Why it paused is said once in chat.
  • Skip and settings only show on hover. Settings stay reachable from the addon list by the minimap and a slash command, so nothing is hover-only.
